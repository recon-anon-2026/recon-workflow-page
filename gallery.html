All 66 reconstructions

Every delivery behind the main results and the transfer ablation, plus the Berliner Dom model shown in the paper, rendered the same way from its saved Blender file. Each tile shows the reconstruction with the input photo in the corner. Click a tile to compare them side by side.

Renders use one oblique camera per scene, framed on the scene's bounding box; room walls are cut away above 42% of the room height for visibility. Tokens are recorded input plus output tokens (including cached input) for the final delivery lineage, active time omits event gaps longer than 30 min, and GPU hours count Slurm allocations; all are lower bounds. The two Sleeping Beauty versions share one session, so they show the same totals. Telemetry is unavailable for St. Vitus and Sofia. The Arc de Triomphe tile shows the latest drawing-guided model (Nov 9) instead of the audited v0005 delivery; it and the additional Berliner Dom model have no recorded token telemetry.
